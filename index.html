About Enprom

Built in North America, Built for What's Next

Enprom LLC is a Texas-registered process equipment and systems integration company. Founded 10 years ago by a group of process integration specialists with over three decades of hands-on experience across oil & gas, petrochemical, and industrial projects throughout the North America.

We exist because clients told us the same story: too many vendors, too little ownership, and too much left to manage themselves. Enprom changes that by bringing engineering, procurement, fabrication, automation, and project management under one roof.

Supporting projects with the cross support of our six service divisions spanning the full project lifecycle — and extending that capability across the US-Mexico border, where our established logistics and supplier relationships give clients a clear path to reliable equipment and compliance services.

Mexico Market

Serving Mexico with a Clear Channel

The Mexican industrial market is underserved by clear, reliable distribution channels for process instrumentation, valves, and piping. Enprom fills that gap with active export operations and established in-country logistics — no middle-of-the-night uncertainty about where your equipment is.

🚚

Established Logistics Partner

We have a network of custom brokers and warehouses that handles all in-country import clearance under existing trade regulations.

📦

Direct Supplier Access

Process equipment, instruments, valves and piping — sourced in the US, exported cleanly to Mexico.

📝

USD-Denominated Contracts

All agreements, invoices, and payments in USD. No currency risk, no dual-entity complexity for our clients.
